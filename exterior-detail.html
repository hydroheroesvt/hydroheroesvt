Exterior Detailing at Hydro Heroes

Your car's exterior takes a beating from Vermont's seasons — road salt in winter, pollen in spring, bugs in summer, and leaves in fall. Our exterior detailing service goes far beyond a basic car wash to restore and protect your vehicle's finish.

Our Exterior Detail Process

  • Pre-Rinse & Foam Bath: We start with a thorough rinse to remove loose dirt, followed by a thick foam bath that safely lifts grime without scratching your paint.
  • Hand Wash: Every panel is hand washed with premium microfiber mitts and pH-balanced shampoo — no automated brushes that scratch your clear coat.
  • Chemical Decontamination: We use dedicated bug, tar, and iron removers to break down and dissolve stubborn surface contaminants without abrasives.
  • Wheels & Tires: Wheels are cleaned with dedicated wheel cleaner and brushes, and tires are dressed for a rich, satin finish.
  • Dry & Protect: We hand dry with plush microfiber towels and apply a protective sealant to guard against UV rays, acid rain, and environmental damage.

Why Choose Professional Detailing?

Automatic car washes use harsh chemicals and abrasive brushes that cause swirl marks and slowly degrade your paint. Professional hand detailing uses gentle, paint-safe techniques that actually improve your finish over time.

Regular exterior detailing also protects your vehicle's resale value. A well-maintained exterior can add thousands of dollars to your trade-in or sale price.

  • Premium Products: We use only professional-grade, paint-safe products.
  • Attention to Detail: Every panel, trim piece, and emblem gets individual attention.
  • Satisfaction Guaranteed: Not happy? We'll make it right.
