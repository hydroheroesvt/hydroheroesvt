Clay Bar Treatment at Hydro Heroes

Has your car's paint ever felt rough or gritty to the touch, even immediately after a wash? That texture is caused by microscopic contaminants embedded deep into your clear coat. Regular washing only removes surface dirt, but a clay bar treatment physically pulls out these stubborn impurities.

The Clay Bar Process

  • Thorough Wash: We begin with a complete exterior hand wash to ensure no loose dirt remains.
  • Decontamination: We use an automotive detailing clay bar and a high-lubricity detailing spray to gently glide over the painted surfaces, lifting tree sap, brake dust, industrial fallout, and overspray.
  • Wax Preparation: After the clay bar leaves the paint flawlessly smooth, the surface is perfectly prepped and ready to absorb and bond with a protective spray wax or sealant.

Why You Need a Clay Bar Treatment

Skipping decontamination not only makes your car feel gritty, but those embedded particles can slowly oxidize, creating tiny rust spots in your clear coat (especially noticeable on white cars). A clay bar treatment halts this damage and drastically improves the shine and longevity of any wax applied afterward.

Why Choose Hydro Heroes?

  • Safe & Effective: We use the right grade of clay and adequate lubrication to ensure your paint is never scratched during the process.
  • Smooth as Glass: The difference is night and day — your car will feel incredibly sleek.
  • Maximum Protection: Essential preparation for our spray wax or other long-lasting protectants.
