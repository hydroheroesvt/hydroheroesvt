Track Detailing at Hydro Heroes

Window tracks are one of the dirtiest — and most overlooked — parts of your home. Over time, dirt, dead insects, pollen, and moisture accumulate in the channels, making windows harder to open and creating an unsightly mess. Our track detailing service tackles this buildup head-on.

Our Track Detailing Process

  • Dry Debris Removal: We start by removing loose dirt, dead bugs, and debris from the tracks using specialized brushes and vacuum tools.
  • Deep Scrub: We apply our eco-friendly cleaning solution and scrub deep into the track channels, sills, and frame edges to break down caked-on grime.
  • Rinse & Wipe: Everything is rinsed clean and wiped dry with microfiber cloths, leaving your tracks looking like new.
  • Lubricate: For sliding windows, we apply a light silicone lubricant so your windows glide open and closed smoothly.

Why Clean Your Window Tracks?

Dirty tracks aren't just an eyesore — they can actually damage your windows over time. Built-up debris prevents windows from sealing properly, which leads to drafts, moisture intrusion, and higher energy bills. Grime can also corrode aluminum tracks and stain vinyl frames if left untreated.

We recommend track detailing at least once a year, ideally paired with your window cleaning service for the best results and value.

  • Improved Window Function: Clean tracks mean smoother operation and a better seal on your windows.
  • Perfect Add-On: Track detailing pairs perfectly with interior or exterior window cleaning for a total refresh.
  • Satisfaction Guaranteed: If your tracks aren't spotless, we'll make it right.
