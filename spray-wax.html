Spray Wax at Hydro Heroes

A spray wax is the fastest way to add a brilliant, protective shine to your vehicle. Whether you're maintaining a fresh detail or want a quick boost between full wax jobs, our professional spray wax service delivers showroom-level results in a fraction of the time.

Our Spray Wax Process

  • Surface Prep: We ensure your paint surface is clean and free of contaminants before applying any wax product.
  • Even Application: We apply a professional-grade spray wax evenly across all painted surfaces, working panel by panel.
  • Buff to Shine: Using clean, premium microfiber towels, we buff the wax to a deep, mirror-like gloss.
  • Trim & Glass: We ensure no wax residue is left on trim, rubber, or glass — just clean, protected paint.

Benefits of Spray Wax

Spray wax does more than just make your car shiny. It creates a hydrophobic barrier that repels water, making rain bead up and roll off instead of sitting on your paint. This means less water spotting, easier cleaning between washes, and added protection against UV rays, bird droppings, and tree sap.

For best results, we recommend a spray wax every time you get a wash, or at minimum once a month during the warmer months. It's the perfect add-on to any exterior detail.
