Screen Cleaning at Hydro Heroes

Window screens are often the most overlooked part of your home's exterior. Over time, they accumulate dust, pollen, cobwebs, and grime that block airflow and reduce visibility. At Hydro Heroes, we give your screens the thorough cleaning they deserve.

Our Screen Cleaning Process

  • Careful Removal: We gently remove each screen from its frame, labeling them so every screen goes back exactly where it belongs.
  • Deep Clean: Using our professional screen cleaning station, we wash both sides of every screen to remove embedded dirt, pollen, and debris.
  • Rinse & Inspect: Each screen is thoroughly rinsed and inspected for tears or damage before reinstallation.
  • Reinstall: We carefully place each screen back in its original window, ensuring a snug, secure fit.

Why Clean Your Screens?

Clean screens aren't just about appearance — they directly impact your home's air quality and energy efficiency. When screens are clogged with debris, less fresh air gets through on those beautiful Vermont days. Clean screens also mean cleaner windows, since dirty screens constantly deposit grime onto your glass when it rains.

We recommend cleaning your screens at least once a year, ideally in the spring when pollen season begins.

Why Choose Hydro Heroes?

  • Dedicated Equipment: We use a professional-grade screen cleaning station for thorough, consistent results.
  • Damage-Free Handling: We know screens are delicate. We handle every screen with care to avoid bends, tears, or frame damage.
  • Bundled Savings: Add screen cleaning to any window cleaning package for the best value.
  • 100% Satisfaction: If any screen isn't spotless, we'll reclean it on the spot.
