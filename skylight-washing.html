Skylight Washing at Hydro Heroes

Skylights are one of the best features of any home — flooding your space with natural light and making rooms feel open and airy. But because they're exposed to the elements 24/7 and difficult to reach, they accumulate dirt, pollen, tree sap, and water spots faster than any other window.

At Hydro Heroes, we have the tools and expertise to safely clean your skylights without damaging your roof or the glass.

Our Skylight Cleaning Process

  • Assessment: We evaluate the type, height, and accessibility of your skylights to determine the safest cleaning approach.
  • Water-Fed Pole System: For most skylights, we use our professional water-fed pole system with purified water — allowing us to clean from the ground without walking on your roof.
  • Scrub & Rinse: We thoroughly scrub away tree sap, bird droppings, pollen, and hard water deposits, then rinse with purified water for a spot-free finish.
  • Frame & Seal Check: While cleaning, we inspect the frames and seals for any visible damage and let you know if we notice anything that needs attention.

Why Clean Your Skylights?

A dirty skylight can block up to 50% of the natural light it's designed to bring in. Regular cleaning ensures you're getting the full benefit of your skylights — a brighter home, lower electricity costs during the day, and better curb appeal from outside.

We recommend skylight cleaning at least once a year, or twice a year if you're surrounded by trees.

Why Choose Hydro Heroes?

  • Safe & Professional: Our water-fed pole system means no ladders on your roof — safer for us and your property.
  • Purified Water Technology: We use purified, mineral-free water that dries completely spot-free.
  • Fully Insured: Your home is protected while we work on hard-to-reach areas.
  • Bundle & Save: Add skylight washing to your exterior window cleaning for the best price.
